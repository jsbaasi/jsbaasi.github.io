## hole type 1
70cm depth 30cm diameter 4x4 post
concrete vol = hole vol - post vol
= 15750pi - 7000
= 42480cm^3
= 0.04248m^3 (divided by a million)
mult by post # (3) = 0.12744m^3
## hole type 2
90cm depth
~0.053 - 0.056m^3
## collar for crete'd post
